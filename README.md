The Smack Tracker: Clinical Decision Support for Buprenorphine Management
Over one million people use buprenorphine for opioid use disorder treatment. Despite its clinical effectiveness, buprenorphine management remains challenging—patients often find themselves on higher doses than clinically necessary, constrained by conservative dosing protocols designed to minimize prescriber liability rather than optimize patient outcomes.
The Smack Tracker addresses this gap. It provides patients with real-time pharmacokinetic modeling based on buprenorphine's actual half-life (24–42 hours) and absorption kinetics (1.5–4 hours to peak). The app visualizes circulating concentration levels, enables precise dose tracking, and simulates how different dosing protocols affect blood levels—transforming what has been an opaque process into transparent, data-driven decision-making. This empowers patients to have informed conversations with their clinicians about tapering, titration, and optimization.
Market Opportunity & Competitive Position
The buprenorphine treatment market comprises 1–1.5 million patients. A substantial subset actively seeks to reduce their dose but lacks the information needed to do so safely and effectively. No comparable application currently exists. The pharmacokinetic domain knowledge embedded in this app would be extremely difficult for competitors to replicate, creating defensible intellectual property from day one.
